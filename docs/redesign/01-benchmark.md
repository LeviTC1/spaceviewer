# SpaceViewer vs Galaxium — benchmark matrix (pass 1, 22 Nov 2026)

Method: real Google Chrome 1xx driven by Playwright (`frontend/scripts/capture-journey.mjs`), ANGLE Metal on an
Apple M1 Pro, 1440×900 CSS px at devicePixelRatio 2 (Retina), default settings, GPU-accelerated. Video + stills for every
journey are in `tmp/redesign-evidence/{galaxium,before}/` (not committed). **V** = verified by observation/recording,
**I** = inference from code or a single still.

| Area | Galaxium (observed) | SpaceViewer before (observed) | What feels worse | Change |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| First frame | "Loading…" for ~2 s, then a lit, cloud-covered Earth with Moon orbit, labels, star field; sidebar of 10 categories with real thumbnails (V) | `/` is a text dashboard: duplicated nav (sidebar + tab strip + "Active workspace" card), "Panel opened / Search ready" chips, no universe on screen (V) | The universe is not visible in the first minute; the home explains the interface to itself | `/` opens the explorer itself on an art-directed scene; archive search becomes a destination, not the gate |
| Onboarding | None — the scene is the onboarding (V) | Modal "FIRST FLIGHT" card over a blurred canvas on both `/skymap` and `/solar` (V) | Blocks the canvas, duplicated per surface | Removed. One quiet hint line that disappears after first interaction |
| Opening scene honesty | Labels are projected from 3-D positions (V) | 12 opening labels (Sgr A*, SMC, LMC, Pluto…) are **hard-coded CSS spans** — SMC is drawn inside Mars's orbit (V, `GalaxiumSkyMapViewport.tsx` `skymap-launch-context`) | Misleading spatial relationships | All labels projected from the same positions that render the object |
| Coordinates / scale | Physically scaled universe; pulling back from Earth shows Earth shrink, Moon orbit, Alpha Centauri, Milky Way band (V) | Every object placed on a **Sun-centred log-compressed radius** in one float32 scene; layers toggle by camera distance (`visible={d>=14 && d<390}`) (V code + V stills) | Off-centre travel is distorted; pulling back from Andromeda centres on the Sun; layers pop | New camera-relative float64 engine with true positions, reverse-Z depth, apparent-size LOD |
| Star field | Thousands of stars, stable points, brightness by magnitude (V) | Gaia budget capped at **120/260/480 stars** of 15,448; star sprites with diffraction spikes; bright panorama dominates (V code) | Sparse, glittery, no parallax | All loaded Gaia stars as physically positioned points, apparent magnitude computed from the camera, real parallax |
| Exposure | Milky Way recedes near sunlit Earth, appears when pulled back (V) | Gaia panorama at ~same brightness behind a sunlit Saturn and the Sun close-up (V) | Planet close-ups look pasted on a poster | Exposure tied to scene brightness; panorama fades when the camera is near lit bodies |
| Search → select | Click result selects, turns camera to look from current position, opens details at `/obj/<slug>`; Enter does nothing (V) | Enter flies immediately; results list mixes types with chips (V) | — (SV's Enter is better) | Keep Enter-to-fly; add type icons, aliases, keyboard nav; select ≠ travel available both ways |
| Flight | Separate "Fly to"; cold link opens already at target (V) | Exponential lerp of position and target; fixed per-kind distance; straight line through scene (V code); flight to Andromeda passes through a black screen (V) | No sense of departure, direction or approach | Zoom-out → cruise → approach path (van Wijk–Nuij optimal zoom/pan in log-distance), duration by journey type, bounds-based framing |
| Planet rendering | Day/night Earth with city lights, clouds; Saturn with ring shadow on planet and planet shadow on rings (V) | Saturn close-up evenly lit (no Sun direction in SkyMap LOD: `timeDays={0}`, light from a fixed point light) (V) | Flat | Sun-directional lighting in camera-relative space for every body, terminator, Earth night/cloud/atmosphere, ring shadows |
| Moons | Moon systems with orbit lines and labels (V) | Moons hidden unless selected (`buildSceneModel` skip) (V code) | Planet's system not explorable | Major moons always present with orbits, labels by apparent separation |
| Galaxies | Andromeda reached by flight; galaxy panel with morphology, designations (V) | Generic orange particle blob; Local Group sprites (V) | Not recognisable, no inclination | Shared galaxy impostor with morphology, true inclination/position angle, dust lanes; Milky Way model at true scale |
| Details panel | Concise description, facts with icons, satellites as links, designations, Wikipedia, Gallery (V) | Dense dossier with model/provenance chips first (V) | Provenance before meaning | Facts first, provenance on demand, onward routes (moons, neighbours, "where in the Galaxy") |
| Time | Date + hour/day/month/year step, play speeds, "Return to now" (V) | Exists in control rail (V) | Buried | Compact time capsule shared across scales |
| Deep links | `/obj/saturn` cold opens at Saturn, panel open (V) | `/skymap?object=solar:planet:saturn` (V) | Object id leaks catalogue structure | `/explore/<slug>` with optional `t` (epoch) and `cam` (pose); old URLs still work |
| Frame pacing | 16.7 ms p50/p95 idle and orbit; hitches to 0.4–1 s during first visits to new destinations (asset streaming) (V) | 16.7 ms idle/orbit; 0.8 s hitch on first Saturn flight (V) | Comparable | Keep 60 fps, avoid first-flight shader compile hitch with warm-up |

Limitations: automation could not test Safari for Galaxium; Galaxium's "spaceship mode" and Android/iOS apps were not
evaluated; Galaxium's spacecraft and black-hole destinations were only seen as categories.
