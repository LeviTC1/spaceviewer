NASA New Horizons Pluto Global Color Map
----------------------------------------
Pluto uses NASA's New Horizons global colour mosaic. Credit: NASA/JHUAPL/SwRI.
SpaceViewer resized and re-encoded the equirectangular source for its high and
performance browser tiers.

Source: https://science.nasa.gov/resource/pluto-global-color-map/
Usage guidance: https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/
NASA should be acknowledged as the source. NASA does not endorse SpaceViewer.

Project-generated data texture
------------------------------
noise.png contains no third-party image. It is deterministic RGB data emitted
by frontend/scripts/build-solar-texture-derivatives.mjs using xorshift32 seed
0x53505632. The two quality tiers are byte-identical project copies.

Machine-verifiable per-file source hashes, output hashes and transformation
receipts are recorded in /solar-textures/manifest.json.
